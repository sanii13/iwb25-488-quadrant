# 🌿 AyurConnect

**AyurConnect** is a digital platform designed to make Ayurvedic healthcare more accessible and trusted. It connects users with Ayurvedic doctors and provides information on herbal plants, home remedies, and wellness practices promoting a holistic approach to health and well-being.

---

##  Features

- 🔍 **Discover Ayurvedic Doctors** – Browse and connect with trusted Ayurvedic practitioners across Sri Lanka.  
- 🌱 **Explore Herbal Plants & Remedies** – Learn about medicinal plants, their benefits, and simple home treatments.  
- 🧘 **Read Wellness Articles** – Access curated articles on holistic living, mental wellness, and traditional healing.  
- 📅 **Book Consultations** – Easily schedule appointments with certified Ayurvedic professionals.  



**AyurConnect** aims to bridge traditional Ayurvedic wisdom with modern digital technology, making natural healing knowledge and access to experts convenient for everyone. By centralizing authentic information and expert connections, the platform fosters a deeper understanding and trust in holistic wellness.
